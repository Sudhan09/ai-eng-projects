
## Part 4 - streaming (Oct 18, 2026)
- 2 GB big.log: 37,095,391 lines, 49 s, peak RSS 15,480 KB (streamed). Target was < 60 MB.
- ~1 GB log (18,650,168 lines): streamed peak 15,352 KB vs list() peak 4,549,036 KB (~300x more).
- Why memory stays flat: generators hand over one line at a time and forget it; the counters only grow with levels and minutes, not lines. A list keeps every record alive at once, so its memory grows with the file.
